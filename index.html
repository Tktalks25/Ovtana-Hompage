The questions you've been carrying alone

"What do I even make for dinner?"

It's 6pm and you're standing in the grocery store. Ovtana already knows where your body is this week — one answer, matched to your phase, your goals, and what you actually like eating.

"Why does the same workout feel impossible this week?"

Because most training plans were written for male physiology. Ovtana programs your lifts, cardio, and rest around your hormones — so a hard week is planned, not a mystery.

"Why does my calorie goal work one week and fail the next?"

Because it's the wrong number half the month. In your luteal phase your burn rises 200–300 calories — Ovtana moves your target with your body, while other apps hand you one number for the whole year.

"My IUD stopped my period — but my body didn't stop changing."

No bleed doesn't mean no signals. Ovtana reads energy, appetite, and sleep instead of a calendar — so you're never locked out just because you don't cycle.

Same goal. Different week. Different math.

One woman, a 2,000-calorie maintenance with a mild fat-loss goal: her target holds at 1,700 for most of the month, then rises to 2,000 in her luteal phase — because her body's burn does. Ovtana runs this math for you, every day, and re-checks it against your own logs and weight trend. Estimates use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for women; your TDEE is a starting point, not gospel. Sustainable fat loss is a 250–500 calorie deficit; lean muscle gain a 200–350 surplus. Illustrative only — not medical advice.
